## Description

Make space for what matters.

Minus is a private photo and video organizer that helps you work through a crowded library one decision at a time. Swipe left to add an item to Pending, or swipe right to keep it. Nothing is removed immediately—review everything in Pending and confirm only when you are ready.

SMART GROUPS

• Find exact duplicates  
• Group screenshots and burst photos  
• Compare visually similar photos side by side  
• Keep the best shot and clear the rest with confidence

FAST, CONTROLLED CLEANUP

• Organize photos and videos with simple swipes  
• Browse by time or album  
• Review Live Photos, animated images, and videos  
• See how many items you have organized and how much space you have freed  
• Confirm every deletion before it takes effect

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Photo grouping and visual similarity analysis use Apple’s PhotoKit and Vision technologies on your device. Your photos are not uploaded for analysis.

MINUS PRO

Eligible new subscribers can start the annual plan with a 3-day free trial; after the trial, it renews annually unless canceled. The monthly plan also renews automatically, while Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Final prices are shown by the App Store for your storefront.

## App Review Notes

Minus does not require the customer to create an account. To open the purchase screen, launch the app, complete or skip onboarding, open Settings, select Minus Pro, and tap View Plans.

The monthly and annual products are auto-renewable subscriptions. The annual product offers an introductory 3-day free trial to eligible new subscribers and then renews at the displayed annual price unless canceled. The lifetime product is a non-consumable one-time purchase. The Restore Purchases button is available on the purchase screen. Photo classification and visual similarity analysis run on-device, and deletion is staged until the customer confirms it.
